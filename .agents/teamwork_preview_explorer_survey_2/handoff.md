## 2. Logic Chain
1. **Chain for Step 4 Crash**:
   - Observation 1 demonstrates unner.overall_progress and unner.pipeline_finished do not exist on PipelineRunner.
   - Observation 2 demonstrates unner.artifacts.bilingual_script does not exist on ArtifactRegistry.
   - Therefore, invoking POST /api/pipeline/start immediately crashes the runner thread or completion handler with AttributeError, leaving the web UI unable to complete automated processing.

2. **Chain for Step 5 Export Failure**:
   - Observation 3 shows export_mp4() checks for a non-existent cache/speech.wav file.
   - Observation 4 shows export_capcut() fails prerequisite checks (oice_ready and pproved_revision_hash) because state.project is disconnected from pipeline artifacts.
   - Therefore, both MP4 export and CapCut export in server.py currently return dummy / raw video copy outputs rather than true rendered or drafted projects.

3. **Chain for Step 3 Blur Disconnect**:
   - Observation 5 shows no REST routes handle mask persistence on the backend, and the frontend still displays manual numeric inputs.
   - Therefore, any blur rectangles drawn or entered on the web UI are discarded, preventing FFmpeg or CapCut from applying delogo or boxblur filters.

4. **Chain for Step 2 Audio Preview Gap**:
   - Observation 6 shows /api/voices/preview and EdgeTTSProvider are absent.
   - Therefore, clicking "Nghe thử" in Step 2 cannot produce sound until a dedicated preview route and Edge TTS synthesis service are added.

1. The backend architecture of ToolVideo contains high-quality domain and processing services (mask_service, capcut_export, 	imeline_audio, ender_service), but the FastAPI adapter layer (src/vkdub/web/server.py) has critical broken wires that prevent the 5-step pipeline from functioning end-to-end.
